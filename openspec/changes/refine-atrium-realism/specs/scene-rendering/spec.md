# Delta: scene-rendering

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Realistic atrium rendering
The system SHALL render the atrium shell with textured surfaces, sky visible through the glass roof, sun shadows from roof beams, and lighting under the mezzanine.

#### Scenario: Looking up in walk mode
- **WHEN** the user looks up from the atrium floor
- **THEN** sky is visible through the glass roof and the beams cast shadows on the floor
